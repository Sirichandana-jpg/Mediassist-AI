TITLE: Hydration
SOURCE: General health education
OVERVIEW: Adequate fluid intake helps maintain normal body functions. Fluid needs vary with age, activity, climate, diet, and health status.
TIPS: Drink fluids regularly; pay attention to hydration during heat and physical activity; follow healthcare advice when a medical condition requires fluid restriction.
WARNING SIGNS: Severe dehydration can cause confusion, fainting, marked weakness, or other serious symptoms and requires medical attention.
DISCLAIMER: Educational information only; it does not replace professional medical advice.
