TITLE: General Emergency Warning Signs
SOURCE: General emergency health education
OVERVIEW: Some symptoms require urgent medical assessment rather than routine self-care.
SEEK EMERGENCY HELP FOR EXAMPLES SUCH AS: Severe difficulty breathing; loss of consciousness; new severe chest pain; sudden weakness, facial drooping, or difficulty speaking; severe uncontrolled bleeding; seizures; severe confusion; serious allergic reaction with breathing difficulty or swelling; immediate danger of self-harm or harm to others.
ACTION: Contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department when an emergency is suspected.
DISCLAIMER: This list is not exhaustive and does not replace professional emergency assessment.
