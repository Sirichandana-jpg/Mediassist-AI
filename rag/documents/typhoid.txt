Disease: Typhoid

Overview:
Typhoid fever is a bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi. It is usually spread through contaminated food or water.

Common symptoms:
- Prolonged fever
- Fatigue
- Headache
- Nausea
- Abdominal pain
- Constipation or diarrhoea
- Sometimes a rash

Diagnosis and treatment:
Typhoid can be confirmed through appropriate testing. It can be treated with antibiotics prescribed by a healthcare professional. Antibiotic resistance can make treatment more complicated.

Prevention:
- Use safe drinking water.
- Maintain good sanitation and food hygiene.
- Wash hands with soap and water.
- Follow recommended vaccination guidance.

When to seek care:
Persistent high fever or worsening symptoms should be medically evaluated.
