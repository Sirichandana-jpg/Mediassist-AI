TITLE: Panic Disorder
SOURCE: National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)
OVERVIEW: Panic disorder involves recurrent unexpected panic attacks and ongoing concern or behavioural changes related to them.
PANIC ATTACK FEATURES: Sudden intense fear or discomfort; racing heart; sweating; trembling; shortness of breath; dizziness; chest discomfort; fear of losing control.
SUPPORT: Panic disorder can be treated. A healthcare professional can assess symptoms and recommend appropriate treatment.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: New or severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, fainting, or other emergency symptoms require urgent evaluation.
