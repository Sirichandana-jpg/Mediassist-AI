Condition: Anxiety Disorders

Overview:
Everyone can feel anxious sometimes. Anxiety disorders involve intense or excessive fear or worry that can be difficult to control and may interfere with daily life.

Possible symptoms:
- Excessive fear or worry
- Restlessness or tension
- Trouble concentrating
- Sleep problems
- Sweating or trembling
- Heart palpitations
- Nausea or abdominal discomfort
- A sense of impending danger or panic

Care:
Effective treatments exist. Psychological interventions such as talk therapy can help people learn coping and problem-solving skills. People with persistent or impairing symptoms should seek professional care.

Important:
Anxiety can occur alongside other mental health conditions. A chatbot should not diagnose a person from text alone.
