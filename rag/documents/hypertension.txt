TITLE: Hypertension
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: Hypertension, or high blood pressure, is a common condition and often has no symptoms.
POSSIBLE SEVERE SYMPTOMS: Severe headache; chest pain; difficulty breathing; vision changes; confusion.
RISK REDUCTION: Reduce excess salt; maintain physical activity; avoid tobacco; follow a healthy diet; maintain healthy body weight; take prescribed medicines as directed.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: Very high blood pressure with severe symptoms requires urgent medical evaluation.
