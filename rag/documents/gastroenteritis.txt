TITLE: Gastroenteritis
SOURCE: MedlinePlus
OVERVIEW: Gastroenteritis is inflammation of the digestive tract, commonly caused by infections.
COMMON SYMPTOMS: Diarrhea; nausea; vomiting; abdominal cramps; fever.
SELF-CARE: Maintaining adequate fluid intake is important.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: Signs of dehydration, blood in stool or vomit, severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, or worsening symptoms require medical evaluation.
