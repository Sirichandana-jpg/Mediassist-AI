TITLE: Influenza
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: Influenza is an acute respiratory infection caused by influenza viruses.
COMMON SYMPTOMS: Sudden fever; cough; sore throat; muscle aches; headache; fatigue.
PREVENTION: Follow recommended influenza vaccination guidance, practice hand hygiene, and reduce exposure when ill.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: Severe or worsening symptoms, especially breathing difficulty, should be medically evaluated.
DISCLAIMER: Educational information only; it does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment.
