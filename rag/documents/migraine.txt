Condition: Migraine

Overview:
Migraine is a recurrent neurological headache disorder. Attacks can be disabling and may last hours to several days.

Common features:
- Moderate or severe headache
- Often one-sided or behind the eye
- Pulsating pain
- Nausea or vomiting
- Sensitivity to light or sound
- Some people experience an aura before an attack

Triggers:
Possible triggers can include sleep disruption, alcohol and certain foods, although triggers vary between individuals.

Care:
Accurate diagnosis and appropriate treatment should be provided by a healthcare professional. Treatment may include medicines and lifestyle measures depending on the diagnosis.

Important:
New, unusually severe or rapidly changing headaches should be medically evaluated.
