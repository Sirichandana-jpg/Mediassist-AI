TITLE: Sleep Health
SOURCE: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI)
OVERVIEW: Healthy sleep supports physical health, emotional wellbeing, attention, learning, and daily functioning.
HEALTHY SLEEP HABITS: Keep a regular sleep schedule; create a quiet sleep environment; limit stimulating activities near bedtime; exercise regularly; discuss persistent sleep problems with a professional.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: Persistent difficulty sleeping, excessive daytime sleepiness, or symptoms interfering with daily function should be evaluated.

DISCLAIMER: Educational information only; it does not replace professional diagnosis or treatment.
