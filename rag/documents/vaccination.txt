TITLE: Vaccination
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: Vaccines train the immune system to recognize and respond to specific infections and can prevent serious disease.
KEY POINTS: Vaccination protects individuals and communities; recommended vaccines vary by age, location, health status, and risk; follow schedules recommended by qualified health authorities.
