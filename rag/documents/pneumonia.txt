TITLE: Pneumonia
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: Pneumonia is an infection of the lungs caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi.
COMMON SYMPTOMS: Cough; difficulty breathing; fever; chills; chest discomfort; fatigue.
PREVENTION: Recommended vaccination, hand hygiene, good nutrition, and avoiding tobacco smoke can reduce risk.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: Difficulty breathing, severe weakness, confusion, or worsening symptoms should receive prompt medical evaluation.
