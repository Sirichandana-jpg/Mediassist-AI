TITLE: Common Cold
SOURCE: MedlinePlus
OVERVIEW: The common cold is a viral infection of the upper respiratory tract.
COMMON SYMPTOMS: Runny or stuffy nose; sneezing; sore throat; cough; mild headache; mild body aches.
SELF-CARE: Rest, adequate fluids, and appropriate symptomatic care can help. Antibiotics do not treat viral colds.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: Seek medical advice for severe, persistent, or unusual symptoms, especially breathing difficulty.
