Condition: Asthma

Overview:
Asthma is a chronic lung disease involving inflammation and narrowing of the airways.

Common symptoms:
- Cough
- Wheezing
- Shortness of breath
- Chest tightness

Management:
Inhaled medicines can control asthma symptoms. Avoiding known triggers can also reduce symptoms.

Important:
Breathing difficulty can become serious. Severe or rapidly worsening breathing problems require urgent medical attention.

Care:
People with recurring asthma-like symptoms should receive professional assessment and an appropriate management plan.
