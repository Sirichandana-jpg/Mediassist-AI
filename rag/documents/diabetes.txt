Condition: Diabetes

Overview:
Diabetes is a chronic disease in which the body does not produce enough insulin or cannot effectively use the insulin it produces. This can lead to elevated blood glucose.

Possible symptoms:
- Increased thirst
- Frequent urination
- Fatigue
- Blurred vision
- Unintentional weight loss

Long-term risks:
Uncontrolled diabetes can damage blood vessels and nerves and increase the risk of complications affecting the eyes, kidneys, heart and other organs.

Prevention and management:
For type 2 diabetes, healthy eating, regular physical activity, maintaining a healthy weight and avoiding tobacco can help prevent or delay onset. Regular medical screening and prescribed treatment are important.

Care:
Diagnosis requires appropriate medical assessment and testing.
