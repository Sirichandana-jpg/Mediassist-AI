Disease: Hepatitis A

Overview:
Hepatitis A is an inflammation of the liver caused by the hepatitis A virus.

Transmission:
It is mainly transmitted through ingestion of contaminated food or water or through direct contact with an infectious person.

Symptoms and course:
Hepatitis A can cause mild to severe illness. Unlike hepatitis B and C, it does not normally cause chronic liver disease, although rare severe complications can occur.

Prevention:
- Use safe drinking water.
- Maintain sanitation and good hand hygiene.
- Follow safe food practices.
- Vaccination is available for prevention.

Care:
People with suspected hepatitis A should seek appropriate medical evaluation.
