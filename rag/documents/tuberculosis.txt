TITLE: Tuberculosis
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: Tuberculosis (TB) is an infectious disease caused by bacteria that most often affect the lungs and can spread through the air.
COMMON SYMPTOMS: Prolonged cough; chest pain; weakness; fatigue; weight loss; fever; night sweats.
DIAGNOSIS AND TREATMENT: TB requires appropriate medical testing and can be treated with recommended antibiotic regimens under professional supervision.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: Persistent cough, coughing blood, unexplained weight loss, fever, or night sweats should be medically evaluated.
