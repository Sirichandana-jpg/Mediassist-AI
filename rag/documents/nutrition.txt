TITLE: Healthy Diet and Nutrition
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: A healthy diet helps support normal growth, development, health, and wellbeing.
GENERAL PRINCIPLES: Eat a variety of foods; include vegetables and fruits; choose appropriate protein sources; prefer minimally processed foods; limit excessive free sugars, salt, and unhealthy fats; maintain adequate hydration.
PERSONALIZATION: Nutritional needs vary with age, health status, activity, and other factors.
