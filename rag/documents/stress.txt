TITLE: Stress
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: Stress is a natural response to challenging situations. Prolonged or intense stress can affect wellbeing.
COMMON SIGNS: Feeling worried or overwhelmed; difficulty concentrating; irritability; sleep problems; fatigue; physical tension.
HEALTHY COPING: Regular sleep, physical activity, social connection, relaxation practices, and seeking support can help.
WHEN TO SEEK CARE: Persistent, overwhelming, or functionally impairing stress may warrant professional support.
