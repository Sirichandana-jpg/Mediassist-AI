TITLE: Hand Hygiene and Infection Prevention
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: Hand hygiene is an important measure for reducing the spread of infections.
GOOD PRACTICES: Wash hands with soap and water when visibly dirty; clean hands during food preparation and eating; clean hands after using the toilet; cover coughs and sneezes appropriately; maintain food and water hygiene.
