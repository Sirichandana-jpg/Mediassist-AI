TITLE: Physical Activity
SOURCE: World Health Organization (WHO)
OVERVIEW: Regular physical activity provides physical and mental health benefits.
BENEFITS: Supports cardiovascular health; physical fitness; mental wellbeing; healthy body weight; and can reduce risk of several chronic conditions.
GENERAL GUIDANCE: Activity should be appropriate for age, ability, and health status. Seek professional advice when health concerns affect exercise choices.
SOURCE URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity
DISCLAIMER: Educational information only; it does not replace professional medical advice.
