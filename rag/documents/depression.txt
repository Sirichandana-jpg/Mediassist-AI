Condition: Depressive Disorder (Depression)

Overview:
Depression is a common mental disorder involving depressed mood or loss of pleasure or interest in activities for a prolonged period.

Possible symptoms:
- Persistent sadness or low mood
- Loss of interest or pleasure
- Changes in sleep
- Changes in appetite
- Fatigue
- Difficulty concentrating
- Feelings of worthlessness or guilt
- Thoughts about death or suicide can occur

Care:
Effective treatment is available for depression. People experiencing persistent symptoms should seek help from a healthcare professional or counsellor and consider talking to someone they trust.

Crisis:
If someone has thoughts of suicide or believes they are in immediate danger of harming themselves, they should contact available emergency services or a crisis line and seek immediate human support.
